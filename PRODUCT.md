# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Iranian and international business owners evaluating a professional website and a longer-term digital operating platform.
- RAVA TEAM customers who need an effective site without becoming responsible for technical provisioning.
- Mohammad Ghasemi and explicitly authorized RAVA personnel who provision, configure, review and operate customer sites.

## Product Purpose

RAVA is a multi-tenant Website Operating System that provisions and manages professional customer websites, beginning with service businesses and later extending to commerce. The RAVA TEAM flagship site must sell this platform, demonstrate the ability required to build it, and present Mohammad Ghasemi's verified design, platform and content capabilities.

The flagship site's primary conversion is a qualified consultation or project-start request. Platform demonstrations and portfolio evidence support that decision rather than competing with it as equal primary actions.

## Positioning

RAVA combines a serious self-hosted website platform, industry-aware starter content, constrained customer-safe management, human review, versioned preview/release/rollback and ongoing service delivery. The flagship site itself must run through the same RAVA customer architecture and serve as Customer Zero rather than as a bespoke marketing bypass.

## Operating Context

- Owner-led customer onboarding and site provisioning.
- Customer content and approved design management inside Template-defined constraints.
- Persian RTL and English LTR experiences.
- Draft → Preview → Approval → Release → Rollback.
- Isolated self-hosted Staging before any separately authorized Production release.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Multi-tenant, permission-aware, entitlement-aware, locale-aware, audit-aware and versioned architecture.
- Services-first product sequence; commerce follows on the shared platform core.
- Templates, Industry Packs, Starter Content Packs and verified Brand Profiles remain independently versioned layers.
- Customers must not receive blank sites or unrestricted controls that can break responsiveness, accessibility, SEO or the Template system.
- The public site must remain manageable through RAVA CMS data and must not become a hard-coded RAVA TEAM exception.
- AI follows Suggest → Diff/Preview → Human Approval → Execute.
- No Production, DNS or `ravateam.ir` change is authorized by design work alone.

## Brand Commitments

- Product and company names: RAVA and RAVA TEAM.
- The experience must meet a strong international quality bar while being intentionally authored for Persian and English audiences.
- Voice must be clear, confident, human and commercially credible; it must avoid inflated technology claims and generic agency language.
- RAVA should feel powerful behind the scenes and unusually simple for customers to use.

## Evidence on Hand

- The implemented RAVA multi-tenant platform and its real workflows are the strongest current product proof.
- Mohammad Ghasemi's content-production capability is a real service area to be demonstrated with approved work later.
- Verified previous portfolio projects, customer testimonials, performance metrics, client logos, awards and detailed career facts have not yet been supplied in a publishable evidence set and must not be fabricated.
- Current illustrative Template media is not automatically proof of client work.

## Product Principles

1. Sell an understandable business outcome, then reveal the platform depth that makes it sustainable.
2. Prove capability through the working product and verified work rather than unsupported claims.
3. Keep customer operation simple while preserving powerful, permission-scoped owner controls.
4. Use one clear conversion path supported by platform and portfolio evidence.
5. Treat Customer Zero as the repeatable delivery model for future customers.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Persian RTL and English LTR must each feel natively authored. Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic structure, reduced motion, readable contrast, touch targets and intentional mobile composition are baseline requirements.
