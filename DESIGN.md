# RAVA Flagship Design System

## Direction

**RAVA Digital Atelier** is the revised visual world for the RAVA TEAM flagship Template. A precise digital page blueprint becomes a complete, living website inside RAVA and reveals that strategy, content, design, technology and safe operation belong to one system.

Physical buildings and architecture photography are excluded from the identity because they misclassify RAVA as an architecture practice. The construction metaphor exists only through real website structure, responsive frames, content layers, preview and release.

## Signature

The first viewport contains one dominant transformation: a real RAVA page wireframe resolves into a polished live customer-site surface while its content, responsive preview and release controls remain visible as one system. The semantic interface remains readable before, during and without motion. No other region competes at the same scale.

## Palette

- `paper`: `#F7F4ED` — warm drafting paper, never yellow vintage paper.
- `paper-strong`: `#EEE9DE` — measured section contrast.
- `ink`: `#071A33` — deep architectural navy for type and dark scenes.
- `ink-soft`: `#45566B` — supporting copy with accessible contrast.
- `blueprint`: `#1557FF` — primary action and construction-line accent.
- `verified`: `#159B66` — real success/verified status only.
- `rule`: `#B9C4D2` — grid rules and quiet structure.
- `glass`: translucent paper/ink surfaces derived from the scene behind them; never a generic white overlay.
- `specular`: restrained cool-white edge light for interactive depth.

No gradient text or generic glow. Glass, refraction and 3D depth are intentional identity materials for navigation, menus, primary controls and the live product assembly—not decoration applied to every content surface.

## Typography

### Persian

- Display: Estedad Variable, 620–760 weight, compact but never below `-0.03em` tracking.
- Body/UI: Vazirmatn Variable, 400–600 weight.

### English

- Display: Manrope Variable, 600–750 weight.
- Body/UI: Inter Variable, 400–600 weight.

Headlines use deliberate line breaks and a maximum display size of `6rem`. Body copy stays within 65–75 characters per line. Utility measurements may use tabular numerals; monospace is not decorative.

## Layout grammar

- Asymmetric 12-column editorial grid with measurable alignment lines.
- The Hero gives the architectural/product artifact more area than marketing copy.
- Sections alternate between wide evidence, quiet explanatory text and exact process structure; they do not become repeated equal card grids.
- Lines, coordinates and dimensions encode responsive/product structure rather than becoming a background pattern.
- Mobile re-composes the drawing into a vertical construction sequence instead of merely stacking desktop columns.

## Navigation

- Desktop Navbar is a custom floating glass instrument with bounded refraction, a crisp specular edge and real separation from the scene; it becomes more compact after scrolling without turning into a flat generic bar.
- Platform and Services use accessible, task-oriented layered Mega Menus with a live product/route preview. Other primary items remain direct links.
- One visually dominant action: consultation/project start.
- Active route has a restrained inset/extruded state. Keyboard focus and open-menu state remain unmistakable without depth or color alone.
- Primary buttons use tactile depth, edge light and short physical press feedback; they are compact, content-sized controls rather than oversized blue rectangles.
- Mobile navigation is a designed full-height refractive sheet with large routes, current-page context and the primary action; it is not a generic dropdown.
- Sticky navigation never obscures focused content and the skip link remains available.

## Motion grammar

- One authored Hero moment: responsive construction lines establish a page frame, semantic content modules lock into place, the finished site resolves, then the RAVA controls and message settle in reading order.
- The transformation may continue through one short scroll-controlled chapter; no more than one pinned section.
- Supporting reveals use small offsets and restrained exponential/ease-out timing.
- Hover and focus stay CSS-driven. Complex choreography uses one animation runtime only if adopted after measured dependency review.
- Reduced-motion mode renders the finished frame immediately and disables scrub, pin and parallax.
- Offscreen/hidden expensive work pauses and every runtime resource is cleaned up.

## Imagery

- The RAVA product and one decisive generated customer-site composition are the Hero focal assets; no physical building imagery.
- Generated imagery is a design material, not evidence of client work, and carries prompt provenance.
- Real portfolio images must be verified, licensed and labelled honestly.
- No fake people, customers, awards, testimonials, logos or performance claims.
- Responsive crops, stable aspect ratios, useful alt text and non-media fallbacks are mandatory.

## Component character

- Buttons are compact tactile controls with purposeful 3D depth; their geometry follows the floating navigation instrument and avoids oversized rectangular CTA treatment.
- Glass surfaces use one coherent optical model: backdrop, border refraction, inner highlight and restrained shadow. Ordinary content surfaces remain quiet and opaque.
- Surfaces use either a fine rule or restrained shadow unless the approved optical glass model specifically requires both edge and depth cues.
- Icons use one consistent professional system and support text rather than replacing it.
- Browser surfaces—selection, focus, scrollbar and underline—inherit the blueprint palette.

## Content and conversion

The primary action is a qualified consultation/project-start request. Platform exploration and verified work are supporting evidence paths. The story order is:

`Business outcome → RAVA mechanism in action → Services and content capability → Verified work → Delivery path → Consultation`

The working RAVA platform is current proof. Future portfolio, testimonials, statistics and client marks remain absent until verified.

## Boundaries

- The public site remains CMS-driven and Template-versioned; no RAVA TEAM-only hard-coded bypass.
- Persian RTL and English LTR are independently composed.
- Accessibility, performance, entitlement, preview, release and rollback rules remain platform requirements.
- The rejected architecture-photo comps and current Horizon/Journey look are anti-references, not visual dependencies.
